In short

You watch a recording of a worker doing a task. Every time the worker has trouble, you write down when it happened, what you saw and why it was a problem.

  1. Watch
  2. Mark the moment
  3. Pick what you saw
  4. Describe the problem

1 How to evaluate a video

  1. Open the video. Go to Videos and click Evaluate. A timer starts; it only counts while the page is open.
  2. Mark when the trouble starts and ends. Press {{ panel_shortcut_labels.mark_start }} at the start and {{ panel_shortcut_labels.mark_end }} at the end. If it was instant, you only need the start.
  3. Choose the problem. Type to find it in the list. If it is new, pick Create new problem and give it a severity and a type.
  4. Say what you saw and where. Pick the indicator (what the worker did or said) and the task moment (which part of the task). Add a comment if it helps.
  5. Save and review. Click Save Annotation. You can check and fix everything in the tables below the video with the button.
  6. Finish. When you are done with the whole video, click Finish Evaluation.

Press {{ panel_shortcut_labels.toggle_guide }} anywhere in the panel to open this guide without leaving the video.

3 Indicators indicator_code

The indicator is the sign that tells you the worker had trouble. Pick the one that best matches what you saw.

What the worker does

ACTWrong action

Does a step in the wrong order, skips it, or does a different step.

DISCDiscontinues action

Starts something and stops halfway, or goes to click and doesn't.

EXEExecution problem

Does the right step, but badly or the long way.

REPRepeated action

Does the same thing again and gets the same result.

CORRCorrective action

Undoes or fixes something they just did.

STOPTask stopped

Moves on to something new before finishing the current task.

What the worker says or shows

GOALWrong goal

Tries to do something the page can't do or that doesn't help the task.

PUZZPuzzled

Doesn't know what to do next or which option to use.

RANDRandom actions

Is just guessing or clicking around.

SEARCHSearches for function

Looks for a button or option and can't find it.

DIFFExecution difficulty

Finds an action physically hard, e.g. a tiny button or a tricky drag.

DSFDoubt, surprise, frustration

Isn't sure it worked, is surprised by the result, or gets annoyed.

RECRecognition of error

Realises they made a mistake earlier, or suddenly understands something.

QUITQuits task

Knows the task went wrong but moves on anyway.

4 Task moments task_moment

Which part of the task the worker was in. They usually appear in this order.

  1. Qualification optionalA test before starting, e.g. a language test or browser check.
  2. InstructionsThe explanation of what to do.
  3. Demographic questionnaire optionalQuestions about the worker (age, country…).
  4. TrainingA practice, demo or quiz to learn the task.
  5. Main taskThe real work.
  6. Final questionnaire optionalQuestions about how the task went.
  7. PaymentInformation about getting paid.
  8. Help/Support/FeedbackAny place to ask for help or send comments.

5 How to write a problem problem

A problem is anything in the task or the page that makes it hard, slow, annoying or impossible for the worker. Write it with one of these two patterns:

Pattern A · start from the page

The what is wrong, so the worker has this trouble.

The instructions have too much text, so the worker skips parts of them.

Pattern B · start from the worker

The worker does this instead of what was expected.

The worker clicks Next instead of reading the instructions.

If you see the same problem again, pick the existing one instead of creating a new one. That is how the summary counts how often it happens.

6 Severity severity

Ask yourself: how much did this hurt the worker?

Critical

It blocked the worker or held them back a lot.

  • Could hardly continue with the task.
  • Didn't understand how to use the information on the page.

Serious

It cost them clear time or made them upset.

  • Lost several seconds.
  • Didn't understand how a feature works.
  • Got clearly annoyed, or thought they broke something.

Cosmetic

A small bump; they kept going.

  • Lost just a few seconds.
  • Did something without knowing why ("you just have to do it").

7 Problem types type

What part of the page caused the problem.

Navigation

Getting lost or not finding the right link or page.

e.g. can't get back to the home page
Layout

How things look or are arranged on the screen.

e.g. the text is too small
Content

Information that is missing, extra or hard to understand.

e.g. an error message they don't get
Functionality

Something is missing or doesn't work as expected.

e.g. no option to show more items per page
