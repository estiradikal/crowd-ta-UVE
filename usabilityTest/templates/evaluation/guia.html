Evaluation Guide

Methodological guide for evaluators.

1. How to evaluate a video

  1. Go to Videos in the sidebar.
  2. Find the video you want to evaluate and click Evaluate.
  3. Watch the video and use the Mark Start / Mark End buttons to capture timestamps.
  4. Fill in the annotation form: indicator, task context, problem description and comments.
  5. Click Save Annotation.
  6. If you identify a general usability problem, fill in the Register Problem form.
  7. When finished, click Mark as evaluated.
